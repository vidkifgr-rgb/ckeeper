A few useful answers

Good to know.

What is C:Keeper?

C:Keeper is an iPhone app for keeping a personal collection of your belongings, purchase details, proof of purchase and warranty dates. Insights help you understand your total investment and the cost of ownership over time.

How do I add my first thing?

Tap the plus button in the app. Enter the item’s name, purchase price, category and purchase date. You can also add a store or seller. The sample collection gives you a starting point to explore the app.

How is the daily cost calculated?

Daily cost gives you a perspective on ownership by spreading the recorded investment over the days owned. For example, a $349 item owned for 426 days costs about $0.82 per day. Recorded care and repair costs may also contribute to the investment shown in the app.

Can I remove the example collection?

Yes. Open the app’s settings and select “Remove example things”. This removes the sample items and keeps the items you added yourself.

Where is my collection stored?

Your collection is stored locally on your iPhone. No C:Keeper account is needed, and the app does not provide cloud synchronization. Your device’s backup settings are managed separately. Keep copies of important original receipts and warranty documents.

Does a warranty date confirm my coverage?

The app helps you keep track of dates and details. Warranty coverage and claim decisions are determined by the seller, manufacturer and applicable law. Check your original warranty terms and retain your proof of purchase.

How can I delete the app’s local data?

To remove C:Keeper and its local data from your iPhone, use Settings → General → iPhone Storage → C:Keeper → Delete App. “Offload App” keeps documents and data. Device backups and any copies you saved or shared separately are managed separately through their respective services. Deleting local data can be permanent; make sure you have kept anything you need.

How can I ask a privacy question?

Email vidkifgr@gmail.com with “C:Keeper privacy request” in the subject. See the privacy policy for details about app data, support correspondence and this website.
