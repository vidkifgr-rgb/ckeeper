1. About this policy

This policy explains how C:Keeper handles information in the iPhone app, when you contact support, and when you visit this website. The app is provided by the C:Keeper developer, who can be contacted at vidkifgr@gmail.com.

2. Information in the app

C:Keeper does not require an account. The collection you create is stored locally on your device. Depending on what you choose to enter, it may include item names, categories, purchase dates, prices, sellers, receipt or warranty details, notes, and care or repair records. This information is used to display your collection, track warranty dates and calculate ownership insights. The developer does not receive your collection from the app. C:Keeper does not send receipts or collection contents to a developer-operated server, and it does not provide cloud synchronization.

3. Analytics, advertising and sharing

C:Keeper does not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not sell or share your collection data. Apple may process App Store, diagnostic or device information under its own policies and the choices you make in your Apple settings. That processing is separate from information you enter into your C:Keeper collection.

4. Storage, retention and your choices

Your collection stays on your device until you remove the data or delete the app. To remove C:Keeper’s locally stored app data, go to Settings → General → iPhone Storage → C:Keeper → Delete App. Offloading the app retains its documents and data. There is no C:Keeper account or developer-held collection to delete.

Your device’s backup settings may cause app data to be included in a device or iCloud backup. This is separate from app-level cloud synchronization. Manage those backups through Apple or your backup provider. Deleting the app does not itself delete existing backups or copies you have exported or shared. Any iOS permissions granted to the app can be reviewed and revoked in the device’s Settings.

5. When you contact support

If you email vidkifgr@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and anything you choose to attach. This information is used to respond to your request, investigate the issue or address your feedback. It is processed through the support mailbox and its email provider, Google, and is not used for advertising. Support correspondence is retained only for as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing the same address. Please do not send sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

6. This website

This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising, cookies for tracking, or contact forms. Fonts, screenshots and scripts are served with the website; it does not load third-party font or analytics services. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even if they are not signed in. GitHub’s handling of website access information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. If you choose an email link, your email app and email provider handle that message under their respective policies.

7. Privacy requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information, object to processing, or make a complaint to a data protection authority. For information held in support correspondence, contact vidkifgr@gmail.com. The developer cannot access or delete the collection stored only on your device; use your device controls to manage that information.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its information practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision. For any question about this policy or the handling of your information, contact the C:Keeper developer at vidkifgr@gmail.com.
